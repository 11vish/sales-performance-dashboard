Sales Performance Dashboard — Executive Summary

Project Overview


This project uses Power BI to analyze sales performance across product categories, regions, and time. The dashboard provides an interactive overview of sales, profit, and order activity.

Key Performance Indicators

Total Sales: 2.33M
Total Profit: 292.30K
Total Orders: 5.111K

These figures represent the overall dashboard totals when no region filter is selected.

Dashboard Features

KPI cards for sales, profit, and orders.
Sales comparison across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
Monthly sales trend visualization.
Sales comparison across West, East, Central, and South.
Interactive Region filtering to explore performance by region.

Business Use

The dashboard helps users compare regional and category performance and examine how sales change over time. Users can select a region to explore the corresponding KPIs and charts.

Conclusion

The report provides an interactive overview of sales performance and supports further investigation into regional differences, product-category contribution, and monthly trends.
